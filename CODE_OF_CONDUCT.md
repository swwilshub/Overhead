# Code of conduct

Overhead is a small, friendly project. We want everyone who plays, reports bugs or contributes to feel welcome.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Assume good intent, and remember there's a person on the other end.
- Disagree about ideas, not people. Explain your reasons and listen to theirs.
- Welcome newcomers and questions of every level.
- Accept decisions gracefully, even when they go against you.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks, in public or in private messages.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Discrimination of any kind, including on the grounds of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or
  expression, nationality, race, religion or sexual orientation.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub. You can open a private
security advisory on the repository and say it's a conduct report, or message a maintainer directly. Reports are
read only by the maintainers and handled in confidence.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break this code of conduct, in
proportion to what happened.

This code of conduct is based on the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
